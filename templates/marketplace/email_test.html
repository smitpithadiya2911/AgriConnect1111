First Time Setup Guide

To send actual emails via Gmail SMTP, you must configure a Google App Password.

  1. Login to the Google Account for the sender email.
  2. Open Google Account Security Settings.
  3. Enable Two-Step Verification.
  4. Search for and open App Passwords.
  5. Generate a new Gmail App Password (choose 'Other' or 'Mail').
  6. Copy the 16-character password generated.
  7. Paste it into your .env file: EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD=abcdefghijklmnop
  8. Restart the Django server in your terminal!
